A one-star review of Earth by an alien tourist — who still has to admit the food was worth the trip.

The entry, in the alien tourist's voice, is a single sentence:

> Gave Earth one star because the locals worship glowing rectangles and queue for bean water, but the tacos were so good I'd let them abduct me again.

The complaint lands first: the locals worship glowing rectangles (their phones) and queue for bean water (coffee) like it is a sacred ritual. Then comes the turn: the tacos were so good the reviewer would let them abduct him again. One star for the planet, five stars for the food — and a fresh angle on the oldest tourist complaint in the galaxy.

## How to run

```sh
MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./out python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry sentence to `out/entry.txt` (148 characters, no trailing newline).
